Our Love Story

Distance tested their love, faith sustained it, and God led them to forever. ❤️ Who would’ve thought that a simple “Happy New Year!” message would become the beginning of their forever? Their story began when Kenneth greeted Abby on New Year’s Day. A few days later, Abby invited him to join an outing with her college friends. To everyone’s surprise, Kenneth said yes, even though it was his very first overnight trip! Little did they know, that simple “yes” would become the start of a beautiful love story. Before they became a couple, they called each other “Sister,” a playful trend at the time. Their friendship gradually grew into something more, and Kenneth eventually won Abby’s heart. Here’s the plot twist. They were actually classmates in college. Although they shared a few classes, they barely spoke because they were both in different relationships at the time. Years later, their paths crossed again, proving that God’s timing is always perfect. Distance became a constant part of their journey. Throughout their relationship, they spent seasons apart while Kenneth focused on preparing for his board exam, later worked in Zambales, and eventually moved to Dubai, while Abby pursued her career in Manila and later in Mariveles. No matter where life took them, they chose each other every single day. Through every video call, countdown, and heartfelt reunion, they learned that love isn’t measured by miles, but by faith, trust, and commitment. Now, after five wonderful years together, including one year of being miles apart between Dubai and the Philippines, they are finally closing the distance and beginning the chapter they have always prayed for as husband and wife.
